# Team Management

> Your engineering notebook needs to display how you guys function as a team. It obvious that you work together to build a robot but how do you keep the team together for the ride? In business, the three metrics for a well managed team is development time, quality output, and customer satisfaction. But this is robotics, so we'll replace customer satisfaction with money. Showing that you are mindfully aware of your resources (time & money) and are utilizing them knowingly demonstrates good team management. Effectively is a hindsight metric since you never know what could take up more time and/or money. Here are some ways you can show team management:

## **Financial Budgeting**

> Team budgeting usually doesn't apply for school teams where members take money for granted. However, private teams who are solely funded by themselves and need to raise funding have a level of professional whim that comes documenting their budgeting. Robotics is expensive and even more eye opening when you track the costs.
>
> Making a page about how money should be spent and where it is going will help a lot, especially if you plan on buying parts yourself or going to signature events. You can show budgets by making a pie chart of spending or logging purchases. These are examples and not the limit, also try to go above and beyond.

&#x20;

## **Inventorying**

> Similarly to budgeting, inventorying is something that school teams usually skip, since they have a seemingly infinite amount of material. But even they can struggle to find the correct material to build (or in the right conditions). Inventorying can contribute greatly in saving time by allowing building members to grab parts before building and confirm the availability of parts. Orders take time to ship so being notified and making an order as soon as possible will avoid stalling your design cycles.

## **Time Management**

> Another team management metric is showing your ability in time management. Many teams rush their design cycle to the last few days before a competition and make critical design mistakes in said rush. Showing time management can be pretty easy. A few examples include adding dates to your notebook pages (please avoid faking these) and using gantt charts to show allocated progress. Highly recommended to use gantt charts to show your engineering design process.
